# Daily append and historic identity: current integration boundary

`inspectDailyAppendSnapshot` is a read-only calculation over a supplied snapshot. It checks the month block, row identity duplication, date and amounts, running balances and subtotal formulae before proposing an ephemeral append or expansion location. A separate, default-off `readDailyAppendPreflight` can fetch the bounded full grid twice and check the proposed target's annotations, protection and merges twice. Neither function reserves a location, establishes that an expense is new, or authorizes any mutation. Missing or customized formulas, financial rows with more than one outflow channel, malformed dates, grids over 5,000 rows and several ambiguous layouts are held for review. The reader has mock coverage only; additional provider checks and an independently fenced fresh read are still required before any use by a writer.

`DailyLegacySourceFence` compares ordered expense and personal-use source snapshots. It deliberately emits a `0=1` dispatch predicate: it does not inventory payroll, manual entries or other possible origins, and a tuple that is unique in the covered subset is not proof of unique historical identity. No candidate count grants authority to enroll rows. Source snapshots are transient, but are sensitive and must not be logged or retained as audit values. Real migration/schema integrity and capacity remain to be assessed before rollout.

The DETAIL projection's canonical dispatch and verification remain blocked until independently obtained Google item-set postimage evidence is bound to the same source revision and durable intent. A caller-provided digest is not provider evidence. DAILY dispatch and overall generation completion remain blocked as well.

Before a runtime may be enabled, it still needs trusted full-source historic provenance or a deliberate hold of ambiguous records; an atomic durable sheet-wide identity/append intent and one-shot dispatch with explicit unknown-outcome recovery; verified Google metadata, cell preimages, notes, validation, merges and protections; a cooperative notice and provider-confirmed pause receipt bound to the current leases; formula and subtotal readback after append or expansion; and per-projection source, provider and generation completion evidence. Legacy workers and prior provider requests must be drained. Human cooperation and a timestamped notice do not make Google Sheets a compare-and-swap system, and an offline or frozen display may be stale. These are release conditions, not claims that the current preflight enforces them.

No flag is enabled and no live data, schema, queue, spreadsheet or deployment is changed by this work.
